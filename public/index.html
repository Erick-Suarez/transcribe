💡 Tips for Better Transcription
  • Keep your microphone close to the speaker's mouth
  • Speak clearly and at a steady pace
  • Minimize background noise when possible
  • Take brief pauses before a new person begins speaking
  • Avoid overlapping speech for better accuracy
  • Use Chrome browser for best audio support
  • Ensure stable internet connection for real-time processing
Best Use Case
This application works best in 1-on-1 settings or small group conversations. For large meetings or conferences with multiple speakers, consider using dedicated meeting transcription tools.
