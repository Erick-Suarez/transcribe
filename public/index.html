💡 Tips for Better Transcription ▼
  • Keep your microphone close to your mouth (6-12 inches away)
  • Speak loudly and clearly at a steady pace
  • Minimize background noise when possible
  • Take brief pauses before a new person begins speaking
  • Avoid overlapping speech for better accuracy
  • Use Chrome browser for best audio support
